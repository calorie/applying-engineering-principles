## Engineering principles policy

For production engineering work that can affect architecture, domain boundaries, module responsibilities, data flow, security or tenant isolation, concurrency or failure behavior, frontend state handling, dependency choices, maintainability, observability, compatibility, or rollback characteristics, the `applying-engineering-principles` skill MUST be loaded and applied.

When Superpowers or another process skill applies, follow its priority and workflow first. `applying-engineering-principles` is a domain/policy skill: it constrains engineering decisions inside that workflow and must not replace or duplicate planning, approval, TDD, debugging, review, worktree, commit, or completion-verification procedures.

Do not rely on memory or a summarized version of the engineering principles when the skill is available.
